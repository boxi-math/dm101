## Third-party material

Every course says what it builds on in its own `course.toml` (`[credits]`: `inspired`, and `adapted` when it adapts
openly licensed text); those lines appear on the site footer and on every video end card. The rule for all courses:

- **Inspired by** = we follow only the order of topics and general ideas. Nothing is copied, translated or adapted:
  no lecture text, board work, figures, worked examples, problem sets, exam questions or video clips.
- **Adapted from** = only for CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or MIT material, with credit and a link, and every adapted section
  is marked in the notes. Material under NC or ND licences, or with no stated licence, is never adapted; we only
  link to it.
